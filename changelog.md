Now built on MenuKit and MenuKit: Containers 5.0.0, and needs both at 5.0.0 or newer, below 6.0.0. Requires Inventory Plus 1.6.0 or newer, whose public API this version uses. Pockets now tell MenuKit they are part of your inventory, and the elytra and totem slots declare their own categories so a search for a spare item never treats the elytra you are wearing as one.

Fixes a crash with Inventory Plus 1.4.0 and newer: equipment restock called a method Inventory Plus renamed, and the game threw the moment a totem or elytra needed replacing. Requires Inventory Plus 1.4.0 or newer.

Built against MenuKit 3.0.0. Requires MenuKit 3.0.0 or newer. No feature changes.

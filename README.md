# Inventory Max

Inventory Max adds the inventory features that need the server's help: slots for an elytra and a totem, pockets behind your hotbar, and container locks the whole server shares. It is the companion to [Inventory Plus](https://modrinth.com/mod/inventory-plus), which it needs.

## Features

Equipment Slots add dedicated slots for an elytra and a totem, so you can keep both ready without giving up your chestplate or a hand. They work in survival and creative. Slot contents survive death like vanilla equipment, respect Curse of Binding, and can mend from XP.

Pockets are extra slots behind each hotbar slot that keep their items on the server. Hover a hotbar slot to show its pockets, then cycle them in and out with the Left and Right arrows. They work in survival and creative, and Inventory Plus's Restock and Auto Tool Switch can take from them.

Mend Anywhere lets Mending repair items from XP anywhere in your inventory, not only in your hands and armor.

Shared container locks sit on top of Inventory Plus's own. Inventory Plus locks container slots for you alone. With Inventory Max on the server and "Also keep container locks on the server" turned on in Inventory Plus's Lock groups tab, the container locks you place are also kept by the server. The server enforces them for every player against shift-clicks, drags, drops and swaps, and they survive a shulker box being broken and placed again. The setting is off by default.

HUD indicators show what's in the equipment slots. Every feature has its own tab, with its switch and settings, in Inventory Plus's settings menu, and Inventory Max's config button in Mod Menu opens them.

## Requirements

- Inventory Plus 1.8.0 or newer
- Keybindery 1.1.0 or newer
- MenuKit 6.0.0 or newer and MenuKit: Containers 6.0.0 or newer, both below 7.0.0
- Fabric API

## Compatibility

Minecraft 26.2 with Fabric. Install it on both client and server. The pockets, the Elytra slot and the Totem slot are named slot groups, so other mods that list slot groups show them by name, with all the pockets as one entry called Pockets.
